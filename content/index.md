   Hi, I'm Rachel (Ruiqi) Zhang. I'm an editorial and language professional with a background in literature. My experience spans academic book publishing, content editing, translation, and quality review. 
   
   Currently, I work as an Editorial Assistant at Springer Nature, supporting the development and publication of academic books. My work involves coordinating with authors and editors, managing editorial processes, and helping manuscripts move towards publication. Alongside publishing, I have also worked on language-related projects involving editing, translation, transcription, and quality review.  
   
   With an academic background in English literature and Literature and Modernity, I have a long-lasting interest in literature, science fiction, theatre, and the ways stories take shape across different media.  
   
   Beyond my professional work, I'm someone who enjoys following curiosity wherever it leads. I like taking an idea that catches my attention and seeing where it might go, whether that means exploring a new language, developing a research project inspired by a television series, or venturing into an entirely unfamiliar subject. I'm particularly drawn to independent learning and projects that allow me to explore connections between different fields.  
   
   I value work that keeps me thinking, learning, and discovering new perspectives, and I'm always glad to connect with people and opportunities that open up new intellectual and creative possibilities. If you’d like to connect — whether for professional opportunities, collaboration, or shared interests — feel free to reach out. My email is ruiqizhang1213@outlook.com.
