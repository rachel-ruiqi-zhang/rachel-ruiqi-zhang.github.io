This poem is written in memory of my lost book list which was deleted by myself using my own hands when my mind didn't realise until too late on a tragic day of a Monday, on November 9th, 2026. I mourn for my book list as well as those that I once remembered but long forgotten, and would perhaps never be remembered again. The last line quotes from Vision, the Marvel Superhero (salut to the TV series).

<pre style="white-space: pre-wrap; font-family: 'Times New Roman', Times, 'Nimbus Roman No9 L', 'KaiTi', 'STKaiti', 'SimSun', serif; font-size: 1.15rem; line-height: 1.9; background: none; border: none; padding: 0; margin: 1em 0;">
Mourning for my book list:
It has the past few years of my mind
Of those I still remember
Of those I long forgotten.
A thread to my hidden memories.

Once lies quietly among my chaotic notes--
Now lost,
no, Deleted. Together with the vault.
How I wish my silver hands to become slower,
to have never pressed the button before my mind caught up:
A book list! Still waiting to be savoured!

But like fire consuming,
The deletion depleting,
Another download of the vault will not save it
From its fate of disappearing.
A trace of my life, once a piece of my mind,
lost and never found again.

In devastating grief I write this,
In hope that I never one day forgot this,
The day on which I lost my book list,
By my own hands I put my mind to mist.

A journal thus created,
New sections to be dated,
Books and theatre collated,
Films and music awaited.
What is yearning, if not grief lingering;
"What is grief, if not love persevering."
</pre>
